ChatShelf

A bookshelf for your AI conversations.

ChatShelf adds folders, tags and pinned favourites to supported AI chat websites, so the conversation you need is always two clicks away.

Add to Chrome — free

Your data stays in your browser. No analytics, no tracking. Privacy

What it does

  • Folders and tags

    File any conversation into folders and label it with your own tags.

  • Instant search

    Find conversations by the titles, folders and tags you saved, right in your browser.

  • Pinned favourites

    Keep the conversations you use every day at the top.

  • Private notes

    Add a note to any conversation to remember why it matters.

Frequently asked questions

Which websites does ChatShelf work on?

The supported websites are listed in the Chrome Web Store listing and in the extension’s settings. ChatShelf only runs on those sites.

Does ChatShelf read or upload my conversations?

ChatShelf reads the page only to get the title and link of the conversations you file. It does not copy your messages, and nothing is sent off your device.
