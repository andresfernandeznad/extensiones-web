ChatShelf Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

In short

  • Everything ChatShelf stores stays in your browser. I have no access to it.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads. I do not sell or share data.
  • The only external connection is the Pro license check with Lemon Squeezy, which receives your license key and an instance name.
  • If you do not activate Pro, ChatShelf sends nothing to any server.

1. Data controller

  • Name: TODO_NOMBRE, self-employed (autónomo)
  • Tax ID (NIF): TODO_NIF
  • Address: TODO_DIRECCION, Spain
  • Email: TODO_EMAIL

2. Data ChatShelf stores in your browser

To work, ChatShelf stores on your device:

  • The names of your folders and tags.
  • The title and address (URL) of each conversation you file, and which folders and tags it belongs to.
  • Your private notes (Pro).
  • Your settings.
  • If you activate Pro: your license key and the activation ID returned by Lemon Squeezy.

This data is kept in Chrome’s local extension storage (chrome.storage.local). It is not synced to your account, it is not sent to me or to anyone else, and it is deleted when you uninstall the extension. Because it never leaves your browser, I cannot see, change or delete it: you control it.

3. License check (Lemon Squeezy)

Only if you activate ChatShelf Pro, the extension connects to the Lemon Squeezy license API and sends:

  • your license key;
  • an instance name that identifies this installation: the extension and browser name (for example “ChatShelf Chrome”). It never includes personal data.

Lemon Squeezy replies with an activation ID, which is stored in your browser and sent along with the key when the extension checks that the license is still valid. As with any internet connection, Lemon Squeezy also receives your IP address and technical request data (such as the browser type).

Purpose: to check that the license is valid and to enforce its activation limit. Legal basis: performance of the license agreement (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

4. Purchases

Purchases are made through Lemon Squeezy, which acts as merchant of record and processes your payment data as an independent controller under its own privacy policy. I never see or store your card details.

Lemon Squeezy shares the order details with me (name, email address, country, product, amount and license key). I use them only to provide support, handle refunds and meet my tax and accounting obligations. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and compliance with legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

5. If you email me

If you email me, I use your address and the content of your message only to reply. Legal basis: my legitimate interest in answering your request (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) or, if it is about a purchase, performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

6. What I do not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, pixels or tracking cookies, in the extension or on this website.
  • I do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
  • I do not use your data for advertising or profiling.
  • No remote code: all of ChatShelf’s code ships inside the package published on the Chrome Web Store.

The use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. How long data is kept

  • Data in your browser: until you delete it or uninstall the extension.
  • Order details: as long as needed to support you and, after that, for the periods required by Spanish tax and commercial law.
  • Emails: as long as needed to resolve your request and, after that, while any liability could arise.

8. Recipients and international transfers

  • Lemon Squeezy (United States), for license checks and purchases. International transfers rely on the Chapter V GDPR safeguards that Lemon Squeezy describes in its privacy policy.
  • GitHub (United States) hosts this website on GitHub Pages and may log visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. I have no access to those logs.
  • Public authorities and courts, only where required by law.

9. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 (LOPDGDD), you can at any time exercise your right to:

  • Access: know whether I process your data and get a copy.
  • Rectification: correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: ask me to delete your data when it is no longer needed.
  • Object: object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Restriction: ask me to keep your data but stop using it, in the cases provided by law.
  • Portability: receive the data you gave me in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Not to be subject to automated decisions: I make no automated decisions and do no profiling.

To exercise them, email TODO_EMAIL saying which right you want to exercise. If I have reasonable doubts about your identity, I may ask for additional information to confirm it. I will reply within one month, extendable by two further months for complex requests, in which case I will let you know.

Remember that I do not hold the data stored in your browser: you can view, export or delete it directly from the extension or by uninstalling it.

If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency, AEPD (C/ Jorge Juan, 6, 28001 Madrid, Spain), or with the supervisory authority of your country of residence in the EU.

10. Children

ChatShelf is not directed at children under 14 and I do not knowingly process data from children under that age.

11. Changes to this policy

If anything changes, I will update this page and the date above. If the change is significant (for example, a new external connection), I will also announce it in the release notes before it takes effect.
