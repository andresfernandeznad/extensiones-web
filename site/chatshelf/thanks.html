Thanks for buying ChatShelf Pro!

Your purchase helps ChatShelf keep improving. Activating your license takes about a minute.

How to activate your license

  1. Find the email from Lemon Squeezy with your receipt. Your license key is in it. If you can’t see it, check your spam folder or look it up in Lemon Squeezy My Orders.
  2. Copy the whole key. It looks like this: XXXXXXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXXXXXXXXXX
  3. Open ChatShelf by clicking its icon in the Chrome toolbar. If you can’t see it, open the extensions menu (the puzzle piece icon) and pin it.
  4. Go to Settings → License.
  5. Paste the key into the “License key” field and click Activate.

Done: you’ll see “Pro active” and the Pro features will be unlocked.

If something goes wrong

“Invalid key”
Make sure there are no spaces at the start or end, and that the key is for ChatShelf, not another extension.
“Activation limit reached”
Deactivate the license on another device (Settings → License → Deactivate) or email me.
Connection error
Activation needs an internet connection. Try again once you are online.
