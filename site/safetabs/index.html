SafeTabs

Never lose your tabs again.

SafeTabs saves your open windows and tabs as named sessions, so you can close the browser, survive a crash or switch projects and get everything back in one click.

Add to Chrome — free

Your data stays in your browser. No analytics, no tracking. Privacy

What it does

  • Named sessions

    Save every open window and tab under a name you choose.

  • One-click restore

    Reopen a whole session, a single window or only the tabs you pick.

  • Automatic snapshots

    SafeTabs keeps recent snapshots so you can recover tabs after a crash or an accidental close.

  • Export and import

    Back up your sessions to a file that you keep.

Frequently asked questions

Does SafeTabs upload my tabs anywhere?

No. Sessions are stored only in your browser’s local extension storage. SafeTabs does not connect to any server.

What happens if I uninstall it?

Chrome deletes the extension’s local storage, including your sessions. Export them first if you want to keep a copy.
